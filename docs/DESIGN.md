# Visual direction

The October 2026 refresh follows Bri’s supplied Turnmark concept: cool white surfaces, deep pool blue, lime highlights, swimming photography, compact comparison cards, and a mobile layout with persistent bottom navigation. The pool diagram depicts the physical lengths of 25yd, 25m, and 50m pools to scale; it does not animate a predicted race or fabricate splits. Conversion history starts empty and shows real local activity.

## Hero asset

`public/images/swimmer-hero.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool and optimized to WebP. The image is decorative; all interface copy, controls, logos, data, and diagrams are real HTML/SVG. No third-party image service is called at runtime.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero photograph for Turnmark swimming app. Primary request: premium editorial sports photograph of an adult competitive swimmer swimming freestyle in a sunlit outdoor lap pool, waterline camera, bright crystalline turquoise and deep blue water, white swim cap and dark goggles, anatomically natural freestyle stroke with realistic water splashes. Composition: panoramic 3:1 wide frame, swimmer primarily in right half, quiet pale blue water and bright softly defocused air in left half to place dark headline over it, no other foreground people. Background: softly blurred swimming pool lane ropes and distant modern pool facility. Natural sunlight, high shutter speed, real detailed water, cool blue palette, sophisticated sporting editorial quality. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks. Deliver just the photographic asset, not a website mockup.
